## Version and scientific limits

Finite cross-work comparisons do not resolve all version families. Shared methods,
resources or cohorts must not be counted again as independent inventions or
independent validation. Source-version and passage-only qualifications remain in
the workbook and HTML; a local correction does not certify an entire version.

The review's historical anchors are selective, not proof of global priority.
Citation links are not technical-inheritance links. Ordinal milestone scores are
branch/era-relative judgments, not a universal quality ranking. A mathematical
guarantee, favorable trajectory sample error, simulated search gain, field
observation and operational rescue outcome support different claims.

The draft identifies gaps at behavior-to-process, probability-to-resource,
non-detection-to-posterior and simulation-to-operational-outcome interfaces. It
does not report a new controlled SAR experiment or validate a combined closed-loop
system. There is no blanket requirement to reread every one of the 208 records in
full before reviewing this scoped first draft.
