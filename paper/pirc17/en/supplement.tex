\section{Missing-input and history-feedback limits}
Invalid online numeric features are zero-filled with a validity mask.
Within a frozen conditioner, the induced drift change is
$\Delta b=\theta_{\rm num}\Delta f+\theta_{\rm mask}\Delta m$, not necessarily zero.
The 404/81 valid development origins do not span these missing-input states.
Feature-file counts, cached query counts, absence of an element, invalid
geometry and a failed forecast have different denominators. Full early/late
speed and drift traces and per-feature missingness across every forecast
were not retained; the four position snapshots cannot reconstruct them.
A one-half overlap correlation for constant-noise 10 s secants is a local
calculation, not evidence for the full nonlinear feedback process or a
measured observation-noise model. No long simulations are rerun here.

\section{Independent follow-up designs, not revised evidence}\label{sec:follow-up-designs}\hypertarget{sec:follow-up-designs}{}
These designs do not replace this cohort, alter its registered families, or
repair a retained failure retrospectively. None is executed in this revision.
\paragraph{N01: Task-matched probabilistic reference.}
Use a constant-velocity state $z=(x_e,x_n,v_e,v_n)$ with process noise,
initialised only from the same visible prefix. Choose process and measurement
noise on development data, not this final cohort. Match origin eligibility,
saved clock, four targets, particle count and scoring conventions. Report
ES, mean-position error, marginal coverage and disk area at each target,
paired by recording block; distinguish Monte Carlo repeats from people.
\paragraph{N02: Mode correspondence before parameter mixing.}
Save direction support, component means, weights and uncertainty for each
training, adaptation and meta-task role. Specify a matching rule using
development data only, compare permuted and matched components before
interpolation, and include a no-mixing reference. This tests the specific
index-mixing procedure, rather than treating a heading rank as a shared
behavioural state or validating meta-learning in general.
\paragraph{N03: Calibration of the evaluated distribution.}
On development trajectories evaluate the actual multi-mode, multi-step
position distribution. Keep component assignments and probability weights
consistent between fitting and validation; distinguish marginal disk
calibration from simultaneous path coverage. Freeze tuning before new final
evaluation. Do not tune a single radius multiplier on the current cohort,
whose one-minute 50\% and 90\% behaviours already differ.
\paragraph{N04: Terrain information versus representation and penalty.}
Use a separately registered cohort and design. Remove redundant constant
mask columns or explicitly match their effective intercept penalty; fit
feature scaling on training data only. Compare dimension/penalty-matched
controls and keep mode count, shared drift, timing and map eligibility fixed.
Specify dependent interaction removal in advance. Report configuration
increments unless information, representation and regularisation have
actually been isolated; do not select successful pairs from failed families.
\paragraph{N05: Clock and source dependence.}
First obtain the provenance of relative timestamps and reconstruct a
documented source-to-saved-clock transformation; then check absolute-time
and UTC interpretation separately. If source identities permit, group
near-duplicate routes and repeated visits without using outcomes to choose
clusters, and compare source/route-cluster sensitivity with recording-block
results. Save early/late drift and speed traces and per-feature validity
counts in future runs. These diagnostics cannot be recovered from four
retained position snapshots or certify independent participants.
