\paragraph{From the fitted model to a forecast.}
Each particle starts at $x_0$ and samples a mode from $\pi$; Full keeps
that mode throughout its path. On each event interval $\Delta\leq5$ saved
seconds, write the locally affine drift as $\mathsf A_m x+a_n$, freezing
the non-position feature at the conditional mean. $\mathsf A_m$ is the
transpose of the two position rows of $\Theta_m$; $a_n$ contains the
intercept and frozen solar term. Full uses the registered split kernel:
an exact affine half-drift, an additive noise increment, then another
half-drift. Its matrices and conditional-moment update are
\begin{align}
 H&=e^{\Delta\mathsf A_m/2},\qquad
 J_{1/2}=\int_0^{\Delta/2} e^{v\mathsf A_m}\,dv,\nonumber\\
 F&=H^2,\qquad J=(I+H)J_{1/2},\qquad
 \Omega=H(\Delta Q_m)H^\top,
 \label{eq:main-affine-kernel}\\
 \mu_{n+1}&=F\mu_n+Ja_n,\qquad
 C_{n+1}=FC_nF^\top+\Omega,\label{eq:main-moment-update}\\
 X_{n+1}^{(p)}&=FX_n^{(p)}+Ja_n+Gz_{n,p},\quad
 GG^\top=\Omega,\quad z_{n,p}\sim\mathcal N(0,I_2).
 \label{eq:main-particle-update}
\end{align}
Initially $\mu_0=x_0$ and $C_0=0$. The same noisy path supplies its
positions at all four actual target times. The drift substeps are exact
for frozen affine coefficients; the split covariance generally differs
from the affine-exact integral
$\int_0^\Delta e^{v\mathsf A_m}Q_m e^{v\mathsf A_m^\top}\,dv$.
Evaluating the solar feature at the conditional mean also approximates
the nonlinear full model. In particular,
``FP'' in the saved procedure denotes conditional Gaussian moments,
not a spatial Fokker--Planck PDE solution. Numerical variants use
Euler--Maruyama or affine-exact reference kernels, and MC/CRN variants evaluate nonlinear
conditions along individual paths. Their complete definitions are in the
\supp{sec:method-model}. This construction produces the empirical measures
in Equation~\eqref{eq:forecast-object}; no subsequent radius calibration
is part of the evaluated forecast.
