\section{How to read the record}
This record separates scientific arithmetic, execution completion, document
editing and human acceptance. The 9 October 2026 revision preserves original
results, protocols and all five retained failures. Source unit migration is
recorded in \texttt{revision46-source-migration.json}. Verbatim English and
Chinese pre-revision sources remain \texttt{historical-main-v1.tex}.
Historical pending counts and old declaration flags are dated snapshots,
not today's run queue. No failed forecast was rerun, no successful audit was
repeated, and no new fit, forecast, seed or bootstrap budget was introduced.

There are 46 unique recording-hash blocks and 58 reused origin--mode cases,
not 58 independent records. Historical fields such as ``closed\_blocks'' and
``support\_blocks'' name program groups; their original JSON meaning is not
silently upgraded to participant identity. The 11,368 scoring slots include
11,363 successes and five failures. Required failures invalidate four
family/mode cells, including both primary terrain families.
Individual failure roots are unknown where stacks or resource/exit records
are missing; controller summaries do not prove numerical divergence, OOM,
an exact timeout or a physical outage. Private paths and raw routes are not
included in this public record.

\section{Reproduction levels and preserved decisions}
Document compilation, saved-aggregate rendering, fixed-stream statistical
replay and permission-controlled saved-output audit are separate levels.
The original independent audit and subsequent full public fixed-stream
replay have readable reports and bindings in \texttt{qualification-v1},
\texttt{public-paired-replay-v1} and \texttt{final-results-v1}.
The original 42 review IDs and criteria remain in
\texttt{review-response-v1.json}; \texttt{review-current-v2.md} preserves
the subsequent author classifications, not reviewer acceptance.
This revision's 46-item record must distinguish edits, automated checks,
scientific gaps and genuine permission/acceptance decisions. A completed
editing item is not a solved scientific limitation or an accepted paper.

\section{Publication choices requiring real information}
No author, affiliation, contribution statement, conflict declaration or
ethics identifier is inferred. Route publication and each map layer's
redistribution scope require distinct real permissions. Until confirmed,
only already-public aggregates and anonymous replay records belong in the
PR. Local real-map review material must not be uploaded automatically.
The owner's requested paper revision authorises PR delivery, not fictional
reviewer approval or scientific reruns.
