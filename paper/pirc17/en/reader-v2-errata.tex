reviews. The 9 October public PDFs, including the 77-page Chinese
supplement, remain unchanged in \path{revision46-review-v1}. Corrected PDFs
are separately versioned; the historical review checklist is not rewritten
as if it had always contained the corrected statements.

sentence were erroneous. Execution content beginning \texttt{c3e44393},
the retained runtime settings and the public paired-replay receipt bind
\path{metrics.py} to SHA-256 beginning \texttt{c00059e4}; the common scorer
\path{formal_scoring.py} matches its execution hash beginning
\texttt{d39adc6d}. Its call chain uses the ordered-pair U statistic with

inertial scorer has a separate direct-distance branch. Full hashes and the
two pure source definitions are in \path{reader-v2-score-binding.json}.
This is a source-binding and manuscript correction, not a new raw-array audit
or a recalculation of scientific scores. A synthetic two-particle regression
distinguishes this operator from the empirical-distribution estimator.

G01 now uses the actual built/vegetation/water--wetland/valid-other groups
and no longer claims to depict slope magnitude. The main body adds input
geometry, the ten-configuration design, all 21 score/error contrasts,
calibration/size and illustrative seed-versus-block uncertainty.

black text and a symmetric signed coverage-deviation scale. Table captions
now distinguish historical pending states from completed scalar/audit
receipts; missing second-stream bootstrap details are not reconstructed.

and R10 (missing task-matched probabilistic baseline) explicitly remain

proposed N03, N04 and N01 designs. No such experiment is executed here.

panel and failure examples, while the local layout gives shared legends,
readable labels and differentiated route/disk lines. Maps, coordinates
and private manifests remain outside this public package. True authorship,
external-reader feedback, route permissions and human acceptance remain
