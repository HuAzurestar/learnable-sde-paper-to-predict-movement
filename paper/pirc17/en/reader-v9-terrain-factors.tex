\paragraph{Selected factors, not generic ``water and roads''.}
The road group uses retained Overture road lines. Its query excludes
bridleway, cycleway, footway, path, pedestrian, steps and track classes;
it is not a complete pedestrian network. The river group uses HydroRIVERS
river lines, not all water surfaces, lake shores, fordability or bridge access.
The cover group (saved key worldcover) uses WorldCover pixels; the surface
group uses slope, aspect and normals from the retained DEM rules. Absolute
elevation is a derivation source, not a selected direct conditioner column.
Other path, rail, lake-shore distance, curvature and roughness representations
are not selected in these ten configurations.

\paragraph{Exactly which distances, vectors and history products enter.}
At query position $X$, $d_g$ is the distance in metres to the nearest selected
line point, and $u_g=(u_{g,e},u_{g,n})$ points from the query towards that
point, for $g\in\{\mathrm{road},\mathrm{river}\}$. The provider chooses the
nearest point in its local metric projection and reports its spherical
distance. Both direction components, log-distance and their product enter:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main-map-distance}
 \ell_g=\log(1+d_g/(1\,\mathrm m)),\qquad c_g=\ell_g u_g .
\end{equation}
The vector is not a line tangent or river-flow direction. The two product
columns are not a hard-coded road attraction or river repulsion law.
No direct $u_{\rm river}^{\top}h$, $u_{\rm river}^{\top}v$ or river-distance
times historical-speed input is selected. History velocity still enters
the shared drift; generated-position feedback also couples motion and maps
indirectly.

The four cover indicators $w$ are built (50), vegetated (10,20,30,40,95,100),
water/wetland (80,90), and bare/snow/other finite valid codes. Missing data
are not valid ``other''. The surface vector is
$s=(\beta,\sin\alpha,\cos\alpha,n_e,n_n,n_u)$: slope in radians, downhill
aspect clockwise from north, and the three-dimensional upward unit normal.
Write $a=-(\sin\alpha,\cos\alpha)$ for uphill and $q=(-a_n,a_e)$ for the
chosen signed contour direction. The four selected compositions are exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main-map-compositions}
 c_{\rm road}=\ell_{\rm road}u_{\rm road},\quad
 c_{\rm river}=\ell_{\rm river}u_{\rm river},\quad
 c_{\rm cover}=\ell_{\rm road}w,\quad
 c_{\rm surface}=(h^\top a,h^\top q).
\end{equation}
Only the last composition explicitly combines terrain with history direction;
it includes no slope- or speed-magnitude multiplier and is not all twelve
surface-by-history products. Reversing $q$ reverses the second projection.
Flat-surface aspect is recorded as zero, not evidence of perpendicular travel.

\paragraph{Base, all, LOO and LIO are configuration operations, not factors.}
Let $B$ be the base history configuration and $A$ the all-terrain configuration.
LOO means leave-one-group-out, $A-g$; LIO means leave-one-group-in above the
history base, $B+g$, not a history-free single-factor model.
Table~\ref{tab:main-terrain-configurations} links these operations to result labels.
Every configuration retains history. Removing a group removes all dependent
compositions, followed by refitting its conditioner and diffusion.
